# ADR-128: Surface evidence-bound process improvement proposals through existing governance

**Status: accepted (ratified 2026-11-04).**

## Context

Prepared at the Captain's request on 2026-10-31.

The [Universe announcement](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-universe-2026-evolving-every-layer-of-the-stack/) describes automations proposing improvements from completed work. Fishhawk already has stronger applicable decisions: ADR-082's historian, ADR-085's earned-autonomy recommendations, ADR-121's protected judge, ADR-122's leased self-origination capability, and ADR-125's evidence-bound captain's log.

The extension is the captain-facing path from an operational problem evidenced on one episode (ADR-125 D1), or a confirmed lesson that ADR-125 D5's `lesson_recurred` shows recurring, to a concrete, measurable process change. It must not duplicate those authorities or silently decide ADR-122 D6's still-undecided closed loop. A recurring-interventions projection can point a human at an episode worth a lesson; it is not evidence.

The sources are decided, not built: the audit category registry carries `precedent_surfaced` (`backend/internal/audit/categories.go:623`) and neither `lesson_recorded` nor `delegation_recommended`, and both names occur only under `docs/adr/`. Once ADR-125's `lesson_recorded` record type exists, authorized evidence on one episode can support a manually requested proposal without any projection.

[ADR-127 / #4880](https://github.com/kuhlman-labs/fishhawk/issues/4880) may supply a recurring-intervention projection; this record does not depend on it.

## Options

- **A. Continue manual retros and filing.** Existing route remains valid; repeated problems are easy to lose.
- **B. A bounded bridge read view of evidence-bound proposals, routed through existing records and verbs (recommended).**
- **C. Automatically rewrite workflow rules or start fixes from observed patterns.** Outside this proposal; would bypass human adoption and ADR-122 prerequisites.

## Recommendation

**Option B.**

**D1. Reuse the record and routes.** Ordinary process-improvement proposals use ADR-125's lesson record and its existing classification. A candidate-change description may be attached only to a lesson of class `defect`, `guidance` or `guard`; a `doctrine_question` opens ADR-082's divergence question and a `judgement` carries no candidate change. Earned-autonomy suggestions remain ADR-085 `delegation_recommended` records. The bridge projects these sources; it does not introduce a second approval ledger or grant. D5's suppression and fingerprint rules govern lessons only; an ADR-085 `delegation_recommended` is declined under ADR-085. An observation that cannot meet ADR-125's decision-bearing evidence and historical-scope rules remains a read-only diagnostic on surfaces outside this record, not a fabricated lesson; this view renders only lessons and ADR-085 `delegation_recommended` records.

ADR-125 D1 stays as written: a lesson's evidence stays on one episode. Recurrence across episodes enters this view only as ADR-125 D5's `lesson_recurred` context on a confirmed lesson; it is not evidence. A candidate for a recurring lesson is a new lesson on one episode with its own decision-bearing evidence under ADR-125 D1, naming the prior lesson id in its candidate metadata. A cross-episode pattern with no such source remains a read-only diagnostic; a human may ask for a lesson about a single episode that exhibits it. A derived projection row, including one a later record may add, is not evidence: evidence must resolve to audit entry ids with hashes under ADR-125 D1.

The bridge proposal-review view is a read surface distinct from ADR-125 D4 read-back; it hosts ADR-125 D2's existing non-agent transitions and ADR-125 D3's existing routing verbs and adds no command of its own. It lists `proposed` lessons, and serves the candidate-change description, only to non-agent credentials holding repository plan-approval authority under the ADR-125 D2 credential floor, refusing an agent-kind subject and the `delegated` marker independently, as ADR-126 D5's gate does; an agent-kind or delegated subject is served nothing by this view, and what it reads of a lesson on ADR-125's other surfaces stays ADR-125's own access rule (D4, D6), which this record does not change. The view also lists confirmed lessons of the routable classes (`defect`, `guidance`, `guard`), candidate-bearing or not, to the same audience, since routing needs them. The view's audience applies to every source it renders, and ADR-085 records keep ADR-085's labelling. ADR-125 D4's selection rule is unchanged. This view, the candidate metadata, the exclusion of candidate bytes from ADR-125 D1's per-episode byte cap (D2) and the ADR-125 D8 change (D6) are the whole amendment to ADR-125. ADR-082 is not amended: its rule 6 keeps precedent captain-facing, and this view is captain-facing. The view shares the bridge with sibling views.

**D2. Reviewable content.** Extend a proposed process lesson, at its creation and in the same `lesson_recorded` entry, with a bounded candidate-change description that is immutable thereafter, so confirmation binds exactly what the confirmer was shown: observed pattern, the source ids and hashes (exactly the lesson's ADR-125 D1 `evidence`, or a subset of it; the candidate cites no other entry), affected workflow and configuration revision, concrete proposed change or candidate diff, expected benefit, downside, evaluation population/metric, observation window, baseline, rollback/abandonment condition and, for a successor lesson (D5), the prior lesson id. Which of these fields are required at creation, beyond the prior lesson id (required for a successor lesson) and the baseline (which an investigation proposal without an attributable baseline may omit), is fixed by the candidate schema version recorded with the entry and specified in the owning package README; a candidate missing a required field is refused at creation. Adding or revising a candidate, including for a confirmed lesson with `lesson_recurred` entries, is a new `proposed` lesson on one episode that independently satisfies ADR-125 D1, names the prior lesson id in its candidate metadata, and needs its own confirmation before routing. Recurrence is read-only context, never ADR-125 D1 evidence, and never transfers a prior confirmation. Distinguish measured facts from inferred causes. A proposal without an attributable baseline may be an investigation proposal, never a quantified savings claim. Do not add numeric benefit thresholds until an evaluator is registered. The affected workflow and configuration revision and the declared target (D5) are author-supplied and advisory; they never alter ADR-125's derived `scope` and never classify a change.

The candidate diff is untrusted proposal content, not an executable command or binding instruction. The candidate-change description is rendered as a proposal only on the bridge proposal-review view. A raw read of the `lesson_recorded` entry (audit, MCP, export) carries it as stored only for a subject in the D1 audience; for any other subject it is excluded from every such read, and a complete-chain read is governed by the next sentence; it is excluded from the digest, the handover brief and the ADR-125 D4 read-back payload for every subject; read-back carries the lesson statement and cited ids within ADR-125's bounds. Where the payload is held in the entry, a complete-chain read that would expose candidate content refuses callers outside that audience rather than altering chain bytes; an implementation that holds the body in an access-controlled store with only its identifier and hash in the entry has no such read to refuse. The backend enforces D1's candidate audience on every read path, including ordinary audit reads, MCP reads and exports, and a read whose audience predicate cannot be evaluated is refused, never served. How the payload is stored or filtered is the implementation's, and this record does not fix it. The lesson record and candidate metadata are never retrieved or injected into planner, implement or reviewer prompts (ADR-125 D4). Separately filed human-authored or human-edited issue text follows D3 and the existing untrusted-issue quarantine (`backend/internal/prompt/prompt.go:9184`, `backend/internal/prompt/prompt.go:9222`). The candidate-change description has its own byte cap, a repository declaration with a conservative product default (ADR-125 D9, ADR-110), so a missing declaration reads as the default; its bytes count toward that cap only, not toward ADR-125 D1's per-episode byte cap, and it never counts toward ADR-125 D1's per-episode count beyond the one entry it rides in.

**D3. Human disposition, not execution.** Use ADR-125 transitions and authority for confirm, refute, retire and fold. Confirmation keeps ADR-125 D2's meaning ("worth remembering") and confers no authority. For a proposal carrying a candidate change, routing it to filing is a separate driver act citing the lesson id (ADR-125 D3); it is not approval of a code change, policy diff or experiment. A deferred proposal stays `proposed`, and any reminder is derived at render time, with no write; there is no new delegated acceptance mode.

Link confirmed proposals to the existing filing verbs ADR-125 D3 names and ordinary governed work. The filed item carries the lesson id, class and the filer's own text. Carriage of the lesson id is the filing path's: until ADR-125's server-verified fold link has a typed carrier, the id is body text that the filing verb does not validate (`backend/internal/mcpserver/file_issue.go:29`), so 'confirmed before routing' is enforced only by the view, which offers the filing action solely for a confirmed lesson, and is otherwise procedural. A typed lesson-link field on the filing request would be an amendment of that surface, not made here. The candidate diff and source ids with hashes stay in the access-controlled record (ADR-125 D6); the candidate never carries copied text of a cited entry. A lesson that cites a security-tied entry carries no candidate-change description. Security-tied means an entry that is, or whose payload references, a `category=security` concern, an ADR-117 escape with `category=security`, a `security`-role crew finding or an ADR-126 D1 security finding (ADR-126 D3); an entry whose tie cannot be read is treated as tied. The server refuses a candidate on such a lesson at creation, withholds one from every read once any cited entry reads as tied, and that work follows ADR-126's path. Where such a candidate's payload is held in the entry, a complete-chain read (D2) that would expose it refuses any caller who fails ADR-126 D5's gate, including one in the D1 audience. Text of the candidate enters an issue only when a non-agent filer authored or edited it. An agent-kind subject is never served a stored candidate (D1), but an agent proposer may retain the candidate text it authored under D4 and submit it through the ordinary filing verb. The requirement that a non-agent author or edit candidate text before filing is procedural on that path too; the candidate read restriction does not enforce it. An agent driver routes under ADR-125 D3 citing the lesson id, and what any agent files is ordinary agent-originated intake under ADR-101 D6. The server cannot verify the authorship of a non-agent filer's text, or recognise a candidate filed without its lesson id, so those two paths are a procedural control and a named residual. The filed item is ordinary untrusted intake to the plan stage, never a pre-approved plan.

An autonomy change goes exclusively through ADR-085 and its human-authored spec change. Protected instruction, policy or judge changes obey ADR-121's tier-I/tier-M handling, classified by ADR-121 D3 on the complete resulting diff; a proposal's declared target is advisory and never classifies a change. Reviewer changes obey ADR-118. The view cannot infer that a human confirmed an exact implementation plan.

**D4. Origination is explicit.** Initial delivery is read-only aggregation. This record adds no server-side model call (deferred; see Consequences): a candidate is written in the same `lesson_recorded` entry as its lesson (D2), by an identity already permitted under ADR-125 D2, with `author` and `actor_kind` derived by the server. An item filed from it carries no `self_improvement/<class>` origin. Origin follows ADR-101 D6 as written: an agent filing, including one through a human credential that is recorded as agent-originated, is ineligible for charter intake; the author of a human-routed filing is whoever ADR-101 D6 assigns (the principal on a filing approval where one exists, otherwise the forge author, with unknown provenance going to captain review), and this record adds no authorship rule. That a human may file an agent's candidate with trivial edits is a residual bounded by ADR-101 D6's per-item attested-digest approval. Autonomous origination of new work remains gated by ADR-122's opt-in lease, per-item principal approval, backlog health, reservations, budgets and registered judge. This record does not implement ADR-122 D6 or permit background origination on an unchartered repository. Agent and first-officer record writes retain ADR-125's existing authority boundaries.

**D5. Staleness, deduplication and noise.** Bind each proposal to its source cutoff and to a declared target drawn from a closed, versioned set of server-computable configuration identities that accepted records already carry: ADR-082 rule 4's `runs.workflow_sha` and "the charter revision once E71.2 #3242 binds it at admission"; ADR-085 (d)'s "matrix hash and `workflow_sha`"; ADR-118 D5's "reviewer-config projection hash of each round's CACHED spec". The server, never the author, computes the hash in force at the source cutoff, read from the run that owns the cutoff entry, and records it with the target. A target outside that set, one the server cannot resolve, or no declared target at all, is recorded `target_unverifiable` and its applicability is never shown as current; the source cutoff is the highest sequence among the lesson's cited entries. An identity in the set with no built computation reads `target_unverifiable` until its own record's implementation exists, and this record adds none; the tree holds `runs.workflow_sha` (`backend/internal/postgres/migrations/0001_runs_stages.up.sql:12`) and a resolved-matrix hash in the `delegation_shadow_evaluated` payload (`backend/internal/server/delegation_shadow.go:258`). A candidate aimed at code, documentation or a script has no identity in this set and reads `target_unverifiable` by design; this record does not track its staleness. A changed target marks its applicability stale: the fingerprint version in the owning package README fixes, per target kind, the resolution that supplies the identity in force now, and a target whose current identity cannot be resolved reads `target_unverifiable`, never current. Never retarget it silently. A stale-target proposal, or one raised again after refutation, is a new lesson naming the prior lesson id in its candidate metadata (D2), with its own immutable bindings and its own confirmation before routing. Prior records are unchanged, and superseding one remains a non-agent transition (ADR-125 D2). Lifecycle status remains historical even if its derived applicability becomes stale.

Group related proposals using a versioned, repository-scoped fingerprint of class, declared target and elements of ADR-125's derived `scope`, not author text; which scope elements enter the fingerprint, and how a `target_unverifiable` target groups, are fixed by the fingerprint version in the owning package README. Grouping does not merge evidence or close records. Refutation suppresses re-listing of the same fingerprint and evidence on this view only; the lessons stay in the audit chain and listable on request. A new lesson under that fingerprint is listed by default only on new decision-bearing evidence that meets the material-change threshold, or when a non-agent explicitly requests it. Rephrasing or time passage alone is insufficient. The material-change threshold, the count of candidates shown per view and the cadence at which applicability and grouping are recomputed are repository declarations with conservative product defaults (ADR-110, ADR-125 D9); a generation-cost declaration belongs to the deferred drafting amendment. The product default for material change is at least one new decision-bearing entry id outside the refuted evidence set, on a different episode, under the same fingerprint. Show no proposals when nothing qualifies.

**D6. No measured claim.** Until a later amendment names the link records (Consequences, Deferred), every proposal reads `not_evaluated`. Nothing here derives a verdict, and no proposal reads "improved" on any other basis. A regression noticed by an existing mechanism (for example ADR-118 D5's scheduled check) routes to the Captain and existing recovery/revert procedures; this record adds no detection and the view never reverts. Proposal-carrying lessons are reported as a separate count in ADR-125 D8's measure and are excluded from its confirmed-lessons-per-episode figure. A reason that cites only proposal-carrying lesson ids is excluded from both the numerator and the denominator of the lesson-citation rate that D8 compares with its baseline, and a reason that also cites an ordinary lesson counts once for that lesson, so they cannot keep the log above its retirement bar.

**D7. Confidentiality and portability.** Enforce repository ACLs on every source read and rendered proposal; confidential security material uses ADR-126's authorized path and never leaks via a public issue, fingerprint, count or diff. Use product defaults and repository declarations (ADR-110), not Fishhawk-specific issue numbers or paths in code.

**Validation.** Walk observation → proposal → confirmation → filing → governed change on a non-Fishhawk fixture through the production configuration resolver and consumers. Vary its repository declarations and assert the resulting behaviour changes; also exercise documented defaults and missing-required-declaration behaviour. Cover source hash mismatch, revoked access, repeated rejection, materially new evidence, an unchartered agent trying to originate work, attempted adoption of a candidate diff as an approval, non-selection of `proposed` lessons by read-back, an agent-kind subject served nothing by this view, a cross-episode lesson refused under ADR-125 D1, the field set of a filed item, an author-supplied target hash replaced by the server-computed one, a candidate read by an agent-kind or otherwise ineligible credential through category-specific and unfiltered audit reads, MCP reads and complete-chain or export paths, and a security-classed source revealing nothing beyond class and location references (ADR-126 D3) through the lesson itself, its fingerprint or its counts. An authority-widening proposal renders as data, routes only as an ordinary filed item and grants nothing, with classification left to ADR-121 on the resulting diff. A target outside the closed set, unresolvable or absent reads `target_unverifiable` and is never shown current, and a changed target hash marks applicability stale without retargeting; a confirmed proposal-carrying lesson cited in a waive reason raises the separate proposal count and leaves the ADR-125 D8 lesson-citation rate unchanged; a citation of a proposal-carrying lesson id leaves both the numerator and the denominator of the ADR-125 D8 lesson-citation rate unchanged. Validate that adding or revising a candidate creates a new `proposed` lesson, leaves the predecessor entry byte-identical, and cannot route using the predecessor's confirmation, including when bindings are unchanged and when `lesson_recurred` prompted the successor. A candidate attached to a `judgement` or `doctrine_question` lesson is refused; a candidate on a lesson that cites a security-tied entry (ADR-126 D3) is refused at creation, including a cited entry that is not itself a security record but whose payload references one, and a cited entry whose tie cannot be read; a candidate whose source ids are not all in the lesson's `evidence` is refused; a candidate over its byte cap is refused, and the refusal does not consume ADR-125 D1's per-episode cap. Prove the absence of direct record-to-prompt delivery and of candidate metadata in ADR-125 D4 read-back (counterfactual-testable), and separately exercise the permitted human filing path. Also exercise an authorized agent proposer retaining its candidate text and filing it through the ordinary filing verb with the confirmed lesson id: the item remains ordinary agent-originated intake, is ineligible for charter intake under ADR-101 D6, and reaches any permitted planning path only through the existing untrusted-issue quarantine.

## Decision

**Accepted 2026-11-04** by the Captain (1 of 1; manual ratification path, the ADR-113 D2 verb is not yet built; approver and hashes in the ratification comment). **Option B**, i.e. D1–D7 as stated in the Recommendation, decided now. It amends ADR-125 only as the Relationships bullet states. Cross-episode evidence, server-side drafting, a typed lesson-link field and outcome joins are deferred. Implementation follows ADR-125's record type. This record grants no authority, starts no work and changes no runtime permission.

## Consequences

- **Value:** a problem evidenced on one episode becomes a concrete decision, including when a confirmed lesson that `lesson_recurred` shows recurring prompts a new lesson with its own ADR-125 D1 evidence (D2), while the captain's log retains lessons whose correct home is human judgement. A cross-episode pattern with no such source stays a read-only diagnostic.
- **Costs:** a bridge projection, bounded candidate metadata, candidate-audience enforcement on every read path including, where the payload is held in the entry, a refusing complete-chain read (D2), the target and fingerprint computations and applicability checks (D5), and the ADR-125 D8 count and citation exclusion (D6).
- **Residuals:** inferred causes can be wrong; review itself consumes attention; a steady stream of low-value proposals can increase toil; the server cannot verify the authorship of a non-agent filer's text or recognise a candidate filed without its lesson id, and a human may file an agent's candidate with trivial edits, bounded by ADR-101 D6's per-item attested-digest approval; the lesson id on a filed item is body text the filing verb does not validate, so 'confirmed before routing' is enforced only by the view and is otherwise procedural; an agent proposer may retain the candidate text it authored under D4 and submit it through the ordinary filing verb, so the non-agent editing requirement is procedural on that path too, and what it files is ordinary agent-originated intake under ADR-101 D6; the rule that a candidate never carries copied text of a cited entry is procedural, because a proposer who has seen confidential text could still paste it into a candidate on another lesson; a non-agent credential used by an agent session is served the candidate and the unconfirmed statement, because `actor_kind` is derived from the credential; the audience rule bounds exposure by credential kind, and the candidate remains untrusted data to every reader; under in-entry candidate storage, a complete-chain read by any caller outside the D1 audience is refused once one candidate exists, so chain verification is audience-gated; the access-controlled-store option avoids this.
- **Sequence:** ADR-125's `lesson_recorded` record type, transitions and access control are a hard prerequisite, implemented first. Then expose its `proposed` lessons and manual routing. The ADR-085 source is shown only when its instrument exists, with named unavailability before that. Add candidates written under this record's D4 only after a manual walk: a human-driven pass of ADR-125 `proposed` lessons through this view and manual routing on this repository, before candidate metadata exists. Autonomous origination remains deferred to ADR-122's prerequisites; no new alpha exit criterion.
- **Deferred:** cross-episode evidence, a future amendment of ADR-125 D1 if, after a declared window of confirmed lessons carrying `watch` predicates, `lesson_recurred` proves insufficient to prompt new single-episode lessons; server-side evidence-grounded drafting, a later amendment that must require source prose to reach any model only through the server's established untrusted-data quarantine, keep server-verified identifiers distinct from source assertions, exclude confidential reproductions from ordinary drafting prompts, and validate malicious instructions and forged evidence identifiers in source text, including attempts to escape the quarantine; a typed lesson-link field on the filing request, a later amendment of that surface (D3); and outcome joins, a later amendment that names the link records and their writer, relates them to ADR-125's `folded` link, and consumes only ADR-118 D5 and ADR-122 evaluator contracts, each with its own named prerequisite. Under that amendment a change inside an ADR-122 reserved cell reads `confounded` or `not_evaluated`, and the amendment must validate results from other work or configuration, mismatched windows, changed registrations, missing joins, incomplete lags and confounding, with counterfactuals on the binding checks.
- **Record metadata.** amends: ["ADR-125"]; supersedes: []; applies_to: []
- **Relationships:** amends ADR-125 for the bridge proposal-review view of `proposed` lessons and confirmed lessons of the routable classes, the candidate metadata in the `lesson_recorded` entry (including the prior-lesson reference), the exclusion of candidate bytes from ADR-125 D1's per-episode byte cap (D2), and the ADR-125 D8 change (D6); that is the whole amendment. ADR-082 is not amended. ADR-085, ADR-118, ADR-121 and ADR-122 remain authoritative and are not weakened or replaced.
