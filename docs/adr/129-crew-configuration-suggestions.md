# ADR-129: Recommend crew configurations from comparable evidence, with human-approved experiments

**Status: accepted (ratified 2026-11-04).**

## Context

Prepared at the Captain's request on 2026-10-31.

Copilot Fusion in the [Universe announcement](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/github-universe-2026-evolving-every-layer-of-the-stack/) motivates reducing the burden of choosing models. Fishhawk's opportunity is a repository-specific recommendation grounded in completed work and governance evidence.

ADR-117 already decides concern outcomes, escape attribution and a per-reviewer scorecard. ADR-118 decides paired reviewer-model experiments, configuration-triggered evaluation, live follow-up and human-only reviewer removal. ADR-121 protects the judge; ADR-122 constrains self-originated experiments. These accepted designs must not be advertised as fully implemented merely because their records exist.

This record decides a deterministic, read-only suggestion projection over ADR-117 and ADR-118 evidence, not an opaque runtime router. It adds no write, no launch, no approval and no new capture. Its statuses read sender and corpus facts that ADR-118 D7 places in repository tooling, and no accepted record names a product read for them. Raw model price, concern volume and unadjusted success rates cannot establish which crew is best.

Companion issues: [ADR-127 / #4880](https://github.com/kuhlman-labs/fishhawk/issues/4880) addresses intervention evidence; [ADR-128 / #4881](https://github.com/kuhlman-labs/fishhawk/issues/4881) addresses the proposal view. Neither substitutes for ADR-117/118 evidence.

## Options

- **A. Manual configuration and existing scorecards only.** Safest baseline, but leaves the captain to assemble comparisons.
- **B. Evidence-bound suggestions for controlled configuration experiments (recommended).** Start with reviewer configuration where the evaluation contract is already decided.
- **C. Automatic per-task model routing or budget-driven reviewer removal.** Outside this record; changes execution and review behavior without established evidence or adoption authority.

## Recommendation

**Option B.**

**D1. Recommendation, never dispatch policy.** Present an incumbent/challenger configuration comparison or a proposed experiment. A suggestion never modifies the resolved workflow, selects a replacement model mid-run, changes reviewer attachment, or weakens a permission/gate. It is never a rung of the implement-model resolution ladder (`docs/spec/workflow-v2.md:755`) and never populates the plan artifact's `model_recommendation` (`docs/spec/plan-standard-v1.schema.json:81`), which remains the plan agent's per-run advisory and is outside this record. An outage remains subject to ADR-118's emergency process (D4), never an inferred cost optimization.

**D2. Exact configuration identity.** Bind each suggestion and its evidence to the complete ADR-118 D4 reviewer-configuration projection, by the ADR-118 D5 hash from each round's cached spec, and to the ADR-117 D4 scorecard dimensions. Any other field a comparison might use (resolved model version, reasoning settings beyond `reasoning_effort`, harness or adapter version, prompt and remit digests, instrument version) is reported as `not_bound` unless an accepted record already captures it. Adding capture is a later amendment of ADR-117 D4, not this record's. Evidence is labelled `version_unresolved` whenever its resolved version is `not_bound` or two rounds under one alias carry different recorded versions; on the `claudecode` and `codex` reviewers no record captures the provider-resolved version: the reviewer `cost_recorded` entry (the shape at `backend/internal/cost/README.md:12`, with the reviewer `source` field at `:26`) carries the configured model id only (`backend/internal/claudecode/client.go:440`, `backend/internal/codex/client.go:517`), and `recordReviewerCost` leaves the run's `resolved_model` pin to the stage agent (`backend/internal/cost/README.md:25`). The label limits comparability. Changed bindings re-derive the status (D5); evidence stays historical.

**D3. Compare like populations.** Report per attached reviewer round (ADR-118 D6) with ADR-117 D4's presence-conditioned denominators and per-defect unique catches, with sample size, configuration-specific windows, labelling coverage, attribution gaps and observation lag. Comparisons are labelled unadjusted; task or risk adjustment is deferred (Consequences). Do not score an absent reviewer as a miss, a waived concern as a false positive, or a merged PR as defect-free. Pooling across repositories is out of scope. Observational comparisons motivate experiments; they do not prove causality. Use ADR-118's paired design for reviewer-model changes, which requires a registered corpus for the arm; a repository without one reads `insufficient_data`. Disclose API-versus-production-harness fidelity.

**D4. Quality before cost.** Incumbent live results must show the applicable live metrics below. Separately, where an offline result is available, show both incumbent and challenger from the same ADR-118 paired recording, with its catch and false-alarm results, decision rule, configuration bindings, fidelity note and the bias and contamination statements of ADR-118 D2; a result resting only on real-derived cases is never shown as an incumbent/challenger comparison. Show recorded experiment cost where available. Offline results do not supply live escapes or operational free signals; unavailable fields remain explicitly unavailable.
- adjudicated catches, unique catches, escapes and severity/coverage, with ADR-117's intervals and exclusions;
- round wall-clock and the free signals ADR-117 D4 already lists;
- known inference cost, price snapshot and usage completeness.

Keep ADR-117's output-tokens-per-real-finding metric distinct from whole-change spend: cache behavior and differing workloads make them answer different questions. A cost row with `known_model=false` or `known_usage=false` is excluded from monetary comparisons and reported as incomplete cost coverage, distinguishing unknown pricing from unreported usage; its stored `usd=0` is never summed as a price (`backend/internal/cost/README.md:13-14`). Do not invent one weighted "best model" score. A lens that is the sole coverage of its remit class is exempt from any floor; ADR-118 D6 governs removal and re-scoping as the Captain's decision.

**D5. Evaluation and adoption.** A suggestion is a derived, rebuildable projection over existing records, computed deterministically, with no model call, no new audit category and no second ledger. A challenger configuration enters the projection only through an existing record type that the deferred product evidence contract names. An agent may raise one only as a crew finding or a filed proposal (ADR-121 D5); a human may raise one through any record the contract admits. A filed proposal uses whatever record the proposal path already uses, and this record adds none. A nominating record enters the projection only when it carries the proposed delta as a change to the subtrees of ADR-118 D4's closed reviewer-configuration list, in a machine-readable form; a record that does not yields no suggestion. No existing record type carries such a form today (a crew-finding payload holds only free-text `summary` and `detail` and an enumerated `severity`, `docs/spec/crew-message-v1.schema.json:208-229`; a filing request holds a proposal's content as free-text `summary`, `body` and `sections`, `docs/api/v0.openapi.yaml:1429-1440`), so its definition and the record types that may carry it are part of the deferred product evidence contract, and until that exists no nominating record qualifies. The view writes nothing: routing a prepared experiment proposal to filing is a separate act by a human through an existing filing verb. Each suggestion carries the exact proposed configuration delta, supporting records and missing evidence, each taken from the nominating record or computed from existing records; an expected tradeoff, evaluation path or pre-registration content that the nominating record does not carry is shown as missing evidence, and the view authors none of them. A configuration delta shown by the view is untrusted proposal content. It is shown with the obligations ADR-118 D1 and D5 attach to the change (for a model change, the arm-table change; for any reviewer-configuration change, the pre-registration). The view never originates a run, and its projection is never automatically retrieved or injected into plan, implement or review prompts. A human may separately author or edit a filed proposal describing the change, which enters governed work through the existing filing path and quarantined issue intake.

This record adds no thresholds. The status set is closed for the first slice; the deferred product evidence contract may add `adoption_ready` and `experiment_failed`. A suggestion reads the first status below whose condition holds, in this order:
1. `not_measured`: the candidate has no sender in the evaluation harness (D7), as recorded by whatever source the deferred product evidence contract names for sender and corpus registration; where a repository has no such source, no candidate can be established as lacking a sender, and every candidate reads `insufficient_data`.
2. `insufficient_data`: a D6 runtime input is absent, or the arm has no registered corpus.
3. `experiment_proposed`: the candidate has a sender and a registered corpus, passes D7's provider checks, and the D6 runtime inputs are present.

The first slice never reads `adoption_ready`, `experiment_failed` or "recommended winner", and a recorded ADR-118 result is shown only through the record the product evidence contract names for it; where the contract names none, that column reads `unavailable`. ADR-117 D4's cell minimums govern the live columns, which read `insufficient_data` per cell without changing the status.

D7's provider checks are applied before the order: a candidate that fails them is not shown and reads no status. The order settles any overlap with D3 or D6: a candidate with no sender reads `not_measured` even while a D6 runtime input is absent. A delta affecting several arms takes the earliest status in the order among its arms. `not_bound` and `version_unresolved` (D2) are evidence labels, not statuses.

Initial delivery prepares experiment proposals only, for a human to file through the existing proposal verbs; the view itself writes nothing. Nothing in this view launches an experiment or authorizes spend. An experiment runs only under ADR-118's existing paths: its registration is the ADR-118 D5 pre-registration, there is no second registration document, and ADR-121 D5 governs adoption.

A configuration is adopted only under ADR-121 D5; ADR-118 D5's pre-registration and offline rule are unchanged. The adopted change then receives ADR-118 D5's registered live follow-up: `regress` requires rollback or Captain re-ratification, and `insufficient_data` leaves the change standing on its offline evidence, flagged. Experiment approval alone authorizes no live configuration change.

The experiment definition and the judge's inputs are ADR-121 tier I and the harness code that executes the judgement is tier M (ADR-121 D1). ADR-121 D5 governs how they change, how an agent may implement a change and how it is adopted; this record changes none of that. Reviewer-configuration experiments are ADR-121 tier I and are never chartered under ADR-122; concurrent review changes in one cell are reported as confounded under ADR-118 D5. Where an ADR-122 D5 reservation covers a cell an experiment touches, ADR-122 D5's interference rule applies; reservations between reviewer-configuration experiments outside any such reservation are left to a later decision.

**D6. Incremental scope.** First deliver read-only reviewer suggestions. The ratified product evidence contract deferred in Consequences is a delivery prerequisite of the projection: this record decides the boundaries any suggestion must keep and what the statuses mean, and no first slice is built before that contract exists. At runtime the projection requires ADR-117 D1 judgements, an ADR-117 D4 scorecard that returns cells for the repository (a below-minimum cell satisfies this), the ADR-118 D5 reviewer-config projection hash of each round's cached spec, and ADR-118 D1's evidence schema v3 with the paired-experiment path; while any of these is absent, every candidate not established as lacking a sender reads `insufficient_data`. Escape and live-follow-up columns read `unavailable` until ADR-117's escape record and ADR-118's scheduled check exist; `unavailable` is a column reading, not a status. Planner/implementer suggestions are future work: they require a registered evaluator for completion, rework, acceptance and delayed escapes, and a separately reviewed implementation design. This record creates no default runtime-routing policy and grants no automatic origination authority.

**D7. Boundaries.** Candidate models must pass the deployment's review-stage allowed-model policy where one is configured (`FISHHAWKD_REVIEW_ALLOWED_MODELS`, `backend/README.md:99`; an unset policy accepts any model, `backend/README.md:96`). That policy is enforced at the approval gate only on the resolved review model, the review stage `executor.model` or the `review_model` override, checked against each implement-stage reviewer provider (`backend/internal/server/approvals.go:2938-2944`); no deployed check validates a `reviewers.agents[].model` entry, so for such a candidate the view's check is a pre-filter that adoption does not re-enforce. A candidate must also have the deployment's reviewer-provider credential configured; the evaluation-harness sender fact is read through the contract's source (D5 status 1), and any check beyond that sender fact is the execution contract's, not this view's. The view checks both; a candidate that fails either is not shown, and until the deferred execution contract exists, the operator running an ADR-118 recording checks them by hand. A candidate with no sender in the evaluation harness is `not_measured` (ADR-118 D1) and is not proposed. Any later execution contract must validate the exact adapter, model and endpoint, including ADR-062's region-scoped inference, before any model call, and repository settings cannot widen deployment policy. Security-class concerns, escapes and `security`-role crew findings inform a suggestion only as ADR-126 D3 allow-listed metadata and are never sent to a candidate provider. Real-derived security cases are excluded from experiment corpora until their advisory is published (ADR-118 D2), which ADR-126 currently makes unavailable, so a security-remit suggestion rests only on the synthetic security arms and allow-listed live metadata: it reads `insufficient_data` where the synthetic arm is absent, and the live column reads `insufficient_data` where the metadata is absent. Public projections expose no confidential counts. A caller who fails ADR-126 D5's gate gets security-class concerns excluded from every count, denominator and unique-catch attribution, and sees a constant statement that restricted sources are excluded, with no number, whether or not any such item exists. The first slice renders only on the authenticated bridge, under the authentication tier and repository-visibility gate of the existing repository read routes it sits beside (the point-read DENY convention, `backend/internal/server/repodash.go:23-27`), enforces account and repository access and each source's read restrictions (ADR-117 A1.6's run-bound restriction on `escape_recorded`, ADR-126 D3) before aggregation or rendering, and refuses run-bound subjects. The configuration delta text renders only to a request that is neither agent-kind nor marked delegated, derived as ADR-126 D5's gate derives it; any other subject, including a delegated request, reads bindings and status only. Those restrictions also govern supporting-record links and caches. It writes nothing to the tracker or site. Validation covers unauthorized repository access, run-bound callers and revoked source access. No cross-tenant training corpus, shared ranking or background paid benchmark is implied by viewing a suggestion.

**Validation.** Test absent reviewers, underpowered cells, unknown pricing and unreported usage excluded from cost sums, provider-version drift, a comparison over differing workloads staying labelled unadjusted, truncated reviewed diffs, delayed defects, a candidate with no evaluation-harness sender reading `not_measured`, a projection never automatically retrieved or injected into a plan, implement or review prompt, a human-filed proposal describing the change entering only through the existing filing path and quarantined issue intake, security-class detail absent from any candidate-provider payload and public projection, and a projection that makes no model call and no write. Test one case per D5 status and their precedence: a fixture meeting two conditions reads the earlier status, and a fixture whose incumbent cells are below ADR-117 D4's minimums still reads `experiment_proposed` with those columns `insufficient_data`. A multi-arm fixture, whose delta affects arms with different statuses, reads the earliest status in the order among its arms. A no-source fixture, a repository with no source for sender and corpus facts, reads `insufficient_data`. Test the provider-check refusal: a candidate outside the review-stage allowed-model policy or without the deployment's reviewer-provider credential is not shown. Test a repository-authorized, non-run-bound operator-agent subject, first-officer subject and delegated request: each receives bindings and status only, with no delta text or supporting-record content, including after a non-agent caller has populated the cache. Removing the agent-audience filter must fail this fixture. No fixture can read `adoption_ready`. A positive fixture must join a nominated delta, a registered corpus and the incumbent's scorecard cells (a cell below its minimum still reads `insufficient_data` and does not change the status) to the exact configuration bindings and produce `experiment_proposed`. Mutating the delta, the projection or the corpus must change the result. A non-Fishhawk fixture exercises the production configuration resolver and suggestion projection using synthetic ADR-117 records. Its live-column readings change when repository-declared cell minimums or windows change, and it reads `insufficient_data` when a D6 runtime input is absent. This tests the projection against the ratified contract (D6) and does not stand in for it. Rendering a suggestion must leave the live configuration unchanged, launch no experiment and authorize no spend. A suggestion appears in no rung of the implement-model resolution ladder and never populates the plan artifact's `model_recommendation`. Evidence whose resolved version is `not_bound` reads `version_unresolved`, and a synthetic fixture whose rounds under one alias carry different recorded versions reads `version_unresolved` as well.

## Decision

**Accepted 2026-11-04** by the Captain (1 of 1; manual ratification path, the ADR-113 D2 verb is not yet built; approver and hashes in the ratification comment). **Option B**, narrowed: D1–D7 as stated in the Recommendation are decided as the boundaries and status meanings of a deterministic, read-only suggestion projection over ADR-117 and ADR-118 evidence. The product evidence contract is a delivery prerequisite, and no first slice is built before it is ratified. The experiment execution contract, declining a suggestion, task or risk adjustment, and the `adoption_ready` and `experiment_failed` statuses are not decided. This record launches nothing, approves nothing and changes no runtime permission. The delta-text audience is ADR-126 D5's full gate predicate, including its credential-kind leg.

## Consequences

- **Value:** makes agent agnosticism useful to the captain without treating multiple models as interchangeable.
- **Costs:** evidence joins and delayed outcomes. Experiment registrations and evaluation spend stay under ADR-118, incurred only when an experiment runs on its paths.
- **Residuals:** selection bias, public-corpus contamination, provider drift and low sample sizes. Some repositories may never accumulate enough evidence for a credible winner. Until a nominating record carries a structured delta, the view may show no suggestion. The D7 allowed-model policy is optional and an unset policy accepts any model, so that leg may not fire on a default deployment; the D7 credential leg always fires, so a challenger whose provider the deployment has not configured is never shown, even though the ADR-118 experiment it would propose runs under the harness's own adapter and key, and configuring that provider is an operator step the view cannot prompt.
- **Sequence:** the ratified product evidence contract is a delivery prerequisite, and no first slice is built before it exists; follow the ADR-117/118 evidence work; start with one reviewer comparison. This is post-alpha depth, not an alpha blocker or a new model harness.
- **Relationships:** amends ADR-118 by adding a decision of its own in ADR-118's area: a deterministic, read-only suggestion surface whose experiment proposals enter ADR-118's existing paths, preserving its decision rule and emergency/removal contracts. ADR-117 supplies measurements and is not amended, ADR-121 protects judges, ADR-122 governs autonomous origination. Supersedes none.
- **Record metadata.** amends: ["ADR-118"]; supersedes: []; applies_to: []
- **Deferred:** named here and not decided. **Experiment execution contract:** approval record, eligible principal, exact revision, budget and population, invalidation and replay, spend, and when the experiment and its pre-registration land relative to the reviewer-configuration change's PR, so that ADR-118 D4's check holds. **Declining a suggestion:** a later record with a human-only predicate, recorded actor kind and delegated flag, keyed to unchanged bindings, leaving the suggestion readable. **Product evidence contract:** how another repository supplies ADR-118-compatible results, given ADR-118 D7's split between tooling and product. The contract covers this repository too: the structured form in which a nominating record carries a configuration delta, and who owns that schema; which record the backend reads for an offline result (for example the ADR-118 D5 registration entry's path and commit) and for an arm's sender and corpus facts (the inputs of D5 statuses 1–3, which ADR-118 D7 places in repository tooling), the mapping from a delta to its affected arms, who owns those reads across ADR-118 D7's split, and the `adoption_ready` and `experiment_failed` statuses. It must decide: which of several registered experiments on one set of bindings decides, and that a re-run needs a new pre-registration; that non-model failures on standing arms count; which cell minimums apply to readiness; and the source record and owner. **Task or risk adjustment of comparisons:** a later decision naming a deterministic stratification over existing records.
